# Glossary of Sri Lankan (Sinhala) Astrology Terms

This glossary collects the core Sinhala and Sanskrit terminology used in Sri Lankan astrology — locally called **Jothisha** (also spelled Jyothishya/Jyotishya) or, in reference to the indigenous Sinhala tradition, **Hela Jothisha**. Sri Lankan astrology follows the broader Vedic (sidereal) astrological system, so most terms are shared with Indian Jyotisha, though a number of Sinhala-specific words and cultural practices (e.g., New Year rituals) are unique to the island.

Terms are listed alphabetically. Where a Sinhala or Sanskrit script form could be reliably sourced, it is included; otherwise only the transliteration and English meaning are given.

| Term | Sinhala / Sanskrit script | English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Avurudda | අවුරුද්ද | The (Sinhala/Tamil) New Year, astrologically significant because its rituals — the lighting of the hearth, first meal, first work, and first bath — are each timed to fall within astrologer-calculated auspicious periods (**Nekath**) marking the sun's transition between the zodiac signs Meena (Pisces) and Mesha (Aries). |
| Bhava | භාව | A "house" — one of the 12 divisions of a horoscope chart, each representing a sphere of life (self, wealth, siblings, family, children, health, marriage, etc.). Houses are counted from the Lagna (ascendant) and each occupies one zodiac sign in the whole-sign house system used in Sri Lanka. |
| Bhukthi / Bukthi | භුක්ති | A sub-period within a planetary **Dasa** (major period); the Dasa lord's overall influence is further divided into Bhukthi periods ruled successively by each of the nine planets, refining the timing of predicted events. |
| Budha | බුධ | The planet Mercury, one of the Navagraha; associated with intellect, communication, and commerce. |
| Chandra | චන්ද්‍ර | The Moon, one of the Navagraha; governs the mind, emotions, and the Rashi (moon sign) used as the primary reference sign in Sinhala horoscope reading. |
| Dasa | දශා | A planetary "period" system (most commonly the 120-year Vimshottari Dasa) that divides a person's life into major periods ruled sequentially by each of the nine planets, used to time when a planet's effects will manifest. |
| Gochara | ගෝචර | Planetary transit — the current, real-time movement of planets through the zodiac, analyzed against the fixed positions of a natal chart to forecast near-term events. |
| Graha | ග්‍රහයා | A "planet" or celestial influencer in astrology; the nine classical Graha (Navagraha) are the Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, and the lunar nodes Rahu and Kethu. |
| Guru | ගුරු (also බ්‍රහස්පති, Brahaspati) | The planet Jupiter, one of the Navagraha; considered the most benefic planet, associated with wisdom, wealth, children, and spirituality. |
| Hela | හෙළ | An ancient/indigenous name for the Sinhalese people and their land (also seen as "Sinhela," "Heladiva," "Helabima"); used as a prefix in "Hela Jothisha" to denote the native Sinhala astrological tradition as distinct from generic Indian Jyotisha. |
| Indul Kiribath | ඉඳුල් කිරිබත් | The ceremonial preparation of milk rice (kiribath) at the astrologically prescribed auspicious time during the Sinhala/Tamil New Year, marking the lighting of the hearth and the first cooking of the new year. |
| Jothisha | ජ්‍යෝතිෂ්‍ය | The Sinhala/Sanskrit term for astrology, literally "the science of light" (from Sanskrit *Jyotisha*); the general name for the astrological system practiced in Sri Lanka. |
| Karana | කරණ | One of the five limbs of the **Panchanga**; a half-Tithi (lunar half-day), of which there are 11 types repeating through the lunar month, used in muhurta (auspicious-time) selection. |
| Kendaraya | කේන්දරය | The horoscope or natal birth chart — a diagram plotting the positions of the Lagna, the 12 houses, and the nine planets at the moment of a person's birth. |
| Kethu | කේතු | The south lunar node, one of the Navagraha; a "shadow" (non-physical) planet associated with detachment, spirituality, and past-life karma, always positioned exactly opposite Rahu. |
| Kuja | කුජ (also අඟහරු, Angaharu) | The planet Mars, one of the Navagraha; associated with energy, courage, conflict, and land/property. |
| Kuja Dosha | කුජ දෝෂය (Mangal Dosha) | An affliction ("dosha") in a birth chart caused by Mars (Kuja) occupying the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house from the Lagna or Moon; traditionally considered a concern for marriage compatibility and checked closely during Porondam matching. |
| Lagna | ලග්නය | The Ascendant — the zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at the exact moment of birth; it fixes the first house of the horoscope and is central to Sinhala chart interpretation. |
| Nakshatra | නක්ෂත්‍රය | A lunar mansion; the 27 (or 28) equal segments of 13°20′ each into which the zodiac is divided based on the Moon's daily motion, used to determine an individual's birth star and for detailed timing and matching calculations. |
| Navagraha | නවග්‍රහ | The "nine planets/seizers" of Vedic and Sinhala astrology: Ravi (Sun), Chandra (Moon), Kuja (Mars), Budha (Mercury), Guru (Jupiter), Sikura (Venus), Shani (Saturn), Rahu, and Kethu. |
| Nekatha / Nekath | නැකත | An astrologically calculated auspicious time (muhurta) for beginning an important activity — weddings, starting work, New Year rituals, and similar significant undertakings are all timed to fall within a Nekatha. |
| Panchanga | පංචාංග | The traditional Hindu/Sinhala astrological almanac, literally "five limbs," combining five daily elements — Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra, Yoga, and Karana — used to identify auspicious and inauspicious times. |
| Porondam | පොරොන්දම් | Horoscope-matching criteria used to assess marriage compatibility between a prospective bride and groom; the traditional Sinhala system evaluates 20 Porondam (such as Dina, Gana, Rajju, and Mahendra Porondam) by comparing both charts. |
| Rahu | රාහු | The north lunar node, one of the Navagraha; a "shadow" planet associated with worldly desire, ambition, illusion, and sudden change, positioned exactly opposite Kethu. |
| Rashi | රාශිය | A zodiac sign — one of the 12 thirty-degree divisions of the ecliptic (Mesha through Meena); in Sinhala astrology "rashiya" most commonly refers to the Moon sign (Chandra Rashi), which is used as the base sign for many predictions. |
| Ravi | රවි (also සූර්ය, Surya) | The Sun, one of the Navagraha; represents the soul, vitality, authority, and father figure in a horoscope. |
| Sade Sati | සාඩේ සාති / ශනි සාඩේසාති | A roughly seven-and-a-half-year period in which the planet Saturn (Shani) transits through the 12th, 1st, and 2nd houses counted from the natal Moon sign; considered an astrologically challenging but potentially transformative phase. |
| Shani | ශනි (also සෙනසුරු, Senasuru) | The planet Saturn, one of the Navagraha; associated with discipline, delay, hardship, longevity, and karmic justice. |
| Sikura / Shukra | සිකුරු | The planet Venus, one of the Navagraha; associated with love, beauty, marriage, luxury, and the arts. |
| Tithi | තිථි | A lunar day; one of the five limbs of the Panchanga, defined as the time taken for the angular distance between the Sun and Moon to increase by 12 degrees, giving 30 Tithi in a lunar month. |
| Vara | වාර | The weekday; one of the five limbs of the Panchanga, reckoned as a solar day beginning at sunrise and named after the Navagraha ruling each of the seven days. |
| Yoga (Panchanga sense) | යෝග | One of the 27 luni-solar Yogas that make up the fourth limb of the Panchanga, calculated from the combined longitudes of the Sun and Moon; distinct from the planetary-combination sense of "Yoga" used in horoscope (Kendaraya) analysis below. |
| Yoga (planetary-combination sense) | යෝගය | In horoscope analysis (as opposed to the Panchanga limb above), a specific combination or placement of planets in a chart believed to produce a defined result — auspicious combinations such as Raja Yoga (power/status) or Dhana Yoga (wealth) are named and analyzed this way. |
