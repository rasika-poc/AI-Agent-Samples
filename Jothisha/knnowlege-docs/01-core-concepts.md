# Core Concepts of Sri Lankan (Sinhala) Astrology — Hela Jothisha

Sri Lankan astrology — known locally as **Jothisha** or **Jyotishaya** ("science of light"), and sometimes as **Hela Jothisha** ("Sinhala astrology") — is closely related to Indian Vedic astrology (Jyotisha). It shares the same sidereal zodiac, the same nine-planet (Navagraha) system, and the same house (Bhava) framework, but it has developed its own regional character over many centuries, shaped by Sinhala-Buddhist culture. It is used widely in Sri Lanka to cast a birth chart (**"jathakaya"**), determine an auspicious time ("**nekath**") for weddings, house-warmings, journeys, and other events, and to counsel on marriage compatibility, career, and health.

This document introduces the basic building blocks a reader needs in order to understand horoscope terminology used in this practice: the planets (Grahas), the zodiac signs (Rashis), the lunar mansions (Nakshatras), the houses (Bhavas), the ascendant (Lagna), and the basic idea of planetary strength (own sign, exaltation, and debilitation).

## Grahas — The Nine Planets (Navagraha)

The word *Navagraha* comes from Sanskrit: *nava* ("nine") and *graha* ("seizer" or "planet/influence"). These are not all planets in the modern astronomical sense — the Sun and Moon are included, and Rahu/Kethu are mathematical points (the lunar nodes) rather than physical bodies — but together they form the complete set of astrological influences used to read a chart.

| Sinhala / common name | Sanskrit | English | What it represents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ravi / Irida | Surya | Sun | Vitality, self, authority, father, soul/ego, status |
| Sandu / Handa | Chandra | Moon | Mind, emotions, intuition, mother, public standing |
| Kuja / Angaharu | Mangala/Kuja | Mars | Courage, energy, conflict, siblings, willpower, land |
| Budha | Budha | Mercury | Intellect, communication, commerce, analytical skill |
| Guru / Brahaspati | Bruhaspathi | Jupiter | Wisdom, expansion, fortune, teachers, spirituality, children |
| Sikura | Shukra | Venus | Love, beauty, relationships, comfort, the arts, marriage |
| Shani | Shani | Saturn | Discipline, delay, hardship, longevity, karma, labour |
| Rahu | Rahu | North Lunar Node | Worldly desire, ambition, obsession, illusion, foreign influence |
| Kethu | Ketu | South Lunar Node | Detachment, spirituality, past-life karma, sudden loss/renunciation |

Each graha "owns" one or two of the 12 zodiac signs and is placed within one of the 12 houses of the chart at the time of birth; the astrologer's task is to read the combined effect of where each graha sits, which signs it owns, and how it relates to the other grahas.

## Rashis — The Twelve Zodiac Signs

A **Rashi** is a 30-degree division of the zodiac belt (the ecliptic), of which there are 12, together making up the full 360-degree circle. Sri Lankan astrology, like Indian Jyotisha, uses the **sidereal zodiac** (based on the fixed positions of stars), which differs from the tropical zodiac used in Western astrology by an offset of roughly 24 degrees (the "ayanamsa").

| # | English | Sanskrit/Sinhala | Symbol |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aries | Mesha (මේෂ) | Ram |
| 2 | Taurus | Vrushabha (වෘෂභ) | Bull |
| 3 | Gemini | Mithuna (මිථුන) | Twins |
| 4 | Cancer | Kataka (කටක) | Crab |
| 5 | Leo | Simha (සිංහ) | Lion |
| 6 | Virgo | Kanya (කන්‍යා) | Maiden |
| 7 | Libra | Thula (තුලා) | Balance/Scales |
| 8 | Scorpio | Vrushchika (වෘශ්චික) | Scorpion |
| 9 | Sagittarius | Dhanu (ධනු) | Archer |
| 10 | Capricorn | Makara (මකර) | Sea-creature/Crocodile |
| 11 | Aquarius | Kumbha (කුම්භ) | Water-bearer |
| 12 | Pisces | Meena (මීන) | Fish |

Each Rashi is ruled by one graha (for example, Mesha is ruled by Kuja/Mars, and Simha is ruled by Ravi/Sun). The sign occupied by the Moon at birth is a person's **Rashi** in everyday usage — often called one's "moon sign" — and is commonly used for predictions and matching in Sri Lankan practice, alongside the ascendant.

## Nakshatras — The 27 Lunar Mansions ("Nekatha")

A **Nakshatra** is one of 27 equal divisions of the zodiac (each spanning 13°20′), tracking the Moon's roughly 27–28 day journey around the earth relative to the fixed stars — the Moon moves through approximately one nakshatra per day. In Sinhala, the nakshatra occupied by the Moon at the moment of a person's birth is called their **"nekatha"** or birth star, and it is one of the most important data points in a Sri Lankan horoscope — often more central to everyday practice (naming ceremonies, matching horoscopes for marriage, choosing auspicious dates) than the Rashi alone.

The 27 nakshatras are: Ashvini, Bharani, Krittika, Rohini, Mrigashira, Ardra, Punarvasu, Pushya, Ashlesha, Magha, Purva Phalguni, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Chitra, Swati, Vishakha, Anuradha, Jyeshtha, Mula, Purva Ashadha, Uttara Ashadha, Shravana, Dhanishta, Shatabhisha, Purva Bhadrapada, Uttara Bhadrapada, and Revati. Each nakshatra has its own presiding deity, symbol, and ruling planet, which colour the personality and life themes associated with a person's birth star. Nakshatras are also subdivided further (into "padas" or quarters) for finer-grained matching and timing work.

## Bhavas — The Twelve Houses

A **Bhava** (house) is one of 12 fixed divisions of the horoscope chart, each governing a distinct area of life. While the Rashis are fixed divisions of the zodiac itself, the Bhavas are divisions of the chart relative to the person's own birth — the first house always begins at the Lagna (ascendant).

| House | Sanskrit name | Life area governed |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Lagna / Tanu Bhava | Self, body, personality, general life direction |
| 2nd | Dhana Bhava | Wealth, family, speech, accumulated resources |
| 3rd | Sahaja Bhava | Siblings, courage, short journeys, communication |
| 4th | Sukha Bhava | Mother, home, comfort, emotional foundation, property |
| 5th | Putra Bhava | Children, intelligence, creativity, romance, past-life merit |
| 6th | Ripu/Shatru Bhava | Enemies, disease, debts, obstacles, daily work |
| 7th | Kalatra/Yuvati Bhava | Marriage, partnerships, business, spouse |
| 8th | Ayu/Randhra Bhava | Longevity, transformation, death, hidden matters, inheritance |
| 9th | Dharma/Bhagya Bhava | Fortune, higher learning, spirituality, father, long journeys |
| 10th | Karma Bhava | Career, status, public reputation, authority |
| 11th | Labha Bhava | Gains, income, friendships, aspirations fulfilled |
| 12th | Vyaya Bhava | Loss, expenditure, isolation, foreign lands, liberation (moksha) |

An astrologer reads a chart by examining which graha sits in which bhava, which graha rules (owns) each bhava, and how those rulers interact — this combination is what produces specific predictions (for example, career potential depends heavily on the 10th house and its ruling planet).

## Lagna — The Ascendant

The **Lagna** (ascendant) is the zodiac sign that was rising on the eastern horizon at the exact moment and place of a person's birth. It is the single most important reference point in the entire chart: the Lagna sign becomes the 1st house (Tanu Bhava), and every other house is counted outward from it in order. Because the Lagna changes roughly every two hours (as the earth rotates), an accurate birth time is essential — even a small error can shift the Lagna and therefore change the entire house framework of the chart.

The Lagna represents the native's body, temperament, physical appearance, and general approach to life, and its ruling planet (the "Lagnesh" or lord of the ascendant) is considered one of the most influential grahas in the whole horoscope, since its placement and condition colour the native's overall life path.

## Planetary Relationships with Signs: Own Sign, Exaltation, and Debilitation

Beyond simply occupying a sign, a graha's *strength* in that sign matters a great deal to interpretation. Three commonly cited conditions are:

- **Own sign (Swakshetra)** — a planet sitting in a sign that it rules is considered comfortable and able to express its natural qualities strongly (for example, Ravi/Sun in Simha/Leo, or Sikura/Venus in Thula/Libra).
- **Exaltation (Uccha)** — each of the seven "true" planets (excluding Rahu and Kethu, which are treated separately) has one sign in which its qualities are considered maximally strong and beneficial. For example, the Sun is exalted in Aries and the Moon in Taurus.
- **Debilitation (Neecha)** — the mirror-opposite condition, in the sign directly opposite a planet's exaltation sign, where its qualities are considered weakest or most challenged. For example, the Sun is debilitated in Libra and Saturn in Aries.

A simplified reference for the seven classical planets:

| Graha | Exalted in | Debilitated in |
|---|---|---|
| Sun (Ravi) | Aries (Mesha) | Libra (Thula) |
| Moon (Chandra) | Taurus (Vrushabha) | Scorpio (Vrushchika) |
| Mars (Kuja) | Capricorn (Makara) | Cancer (Kataka) |
| Mercury (Budha) | Virgo (Kanya) | Pisces (Meena) |
| Jupiter (Guru) | Cancer (Kataka) | Capricorn (Makara) |
| Venus (Sikura) | Pisces (Meena) | Virgo (Kanya) |
| Saturn (Shani) | Libra (Thula) | Aries (Mesha) |

These are broad categories among a fuller traditional scale of planetary dignity (which also includes friendly, neutral, and enemy signs), but knowing just own sign, exaltation, and debilitation is generally enough to follow the basic reasoning behind most horoscope interpretations — a planet that is exalted or in its own sign is read as generally supportive for the house and life area it influences, while a debilitated planet is read as a source of difficulty, though traditional texts also describe conditions ("Neecha Bhanga") under which a debilitated planet's weakness can be cancelled or even reversed into strength.
